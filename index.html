Help

Common questions

We accept JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, and PSD. For best results, upload at least 300 DPI at print size. PNG with a transparent background is ideal for logos. Our system warns you automatically if resolution is too low before you checkout.
Orders are printed and dispatched within 24 hours. Standard delivery is 2–4 working days nationwide. You receive a tracking number via email and SMS once shipped. Express same-day delivery available in Johannesburg for orders placed before 10am.
Yes — all ceramic and glass mugs are dishwasher safe. The sublimation dye bonds into the coating, so it won't chip, fade, or peel. For travel mugs and tumblers, hand washing is recommended to preserve the lid seals and insulation.
Bulk pricing starts from 10 units and you can mix designs within the same order. For 100+ units, contact us directly for a custom quote, dedicated support, and payment terms. We've done runs of 5,000+ for major SA brands.
We stand behind every mug. If there's a production defect or the quality doesn't match the preview, we'll reprint free or give a full refund. No forms, no fuss — just contact us within 14 days of receiving your order.
Yes! Gift wrapping with a personalised message card is available for R25. Select it at checkout. We'll also ship directly to the gift recipient with your message — perfect for surprises. Packaging is discreet with no price info included.
