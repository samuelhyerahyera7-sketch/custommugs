FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Can't find yours? Chat to us on WhatsApp.

We accept JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, and PSD files. For best print quality, we recommend images of at least 300 DPI at print size. PNG files with transparent backgrounds work best for logos. If your file isn't high enough resolution, our system will warn you before you complete your order.
Orders are printed within 24 hours and shipped the next business day. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days nationwide. Express same-day delivery is available in Johannesburg and Cape Town for orders placed before 10am. You'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS.
Yes! All our ceramic and glass mugs are dishwasher safe. The sublimation print is embedded into the coating of the mug, so it won't fade or peel in the dishwasher. For travel mugs and tumblers, we recommend hand washing to preserve the lid seals.
For bulk orders with discounted pricing, the minimum is 10 units. You can mix designs within the same order as long as the total quantity meets the minimum. For orders over 100 units, please contact us for a custom quote and dedicated account management.
We stand behind every mug we print. If there's a production defect or the print quality doesn't meet your expectations, we'll reprint it at no charge. If you approved a design proof and the mug matches the proof, we'll work with you to find a solution. Contact us within 14 days of receiving your order.
Absolutely! If you'd like help creating your design, our in-house designers can help from R99. Send us your brief, logo, photos, and any references, and we'll create a design for your approval within 1–2 business days. Design service orders can be placed via WhatsApp or email.
